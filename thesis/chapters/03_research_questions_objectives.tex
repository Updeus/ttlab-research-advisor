\chapter{Research Questions and Objectives}
\label{ch:rqs}

The research questions span the data pipeline, retrieval and RAG quality, recommendation structure, research discovery and governance, and reproducibility. Each question is answered by a method--evidence--result--conclusion chain rather than by implementation claims alone.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=16mm,style=multiline]
  \item[RQ1] With what coverage, structural quality, and observable failure modes can the catalog snapshot be transformed into an eligible page-aware corpus and complete authoritative indexes?
  \item[RQ2] How do keyword, feature-hashing, learned dense, heuristic hybrid, and development-tuned hybrid retrieval compare on held-out source-derived questions, and which heuristic terms materially change rankings?
  \item[RQ3] To what extent does the offline extractive answerer produce source-supported claims, correct and complete citations, answer-point coverage, and appropriate abstention?
  \item[RQ4] What does AI-assisted proxy review reveal about the evidence fidelity and limitations of thesis-extension recommendations, controlled topic labels, publication-derived author links, and persisted generated outputs?
  \item[RQ5] To what extent is the bounded artefact reproducible and reviewable on documented local hardware, externally format-compatible, and governed by privacy-minimizing, authenticated, accessible evidence interfaces without redistributing restricted PDFs?
\end{description}

\section{Claim boundaries}

RQ2 tests retrieval against AI-silver evidence and does not establish broader-corpus performance. RQ3 tests extractive answers against AI-silver evidence but does not establish human-perceived answer quality. RQ4 combines AI-proxy recommendation, controlled-label, identity, and review evidence; novelty, feasibility, data access, supervisor fit, expertise, and availability remain externally confirmable. RQ5 reports local concurrency-one performance, attributable review and interface controls, restricted-PDF exclusion, and a three-article external format sanity check; it does not establish production capacity or broad generalisation.
