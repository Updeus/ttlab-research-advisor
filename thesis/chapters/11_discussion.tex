\section{Traceability is an engineering property, not an accuracy score}

The platform now preserves paper, chunk, page/section, extraction/chunk/index generation, provider/configuration, warning, timestamp, and review state through its principal workflows. Validated active-generation pointers eliminate the earlier ambiguity between index files and stale database flags. These are meaningful engineering results because they make an output auditable and stale derived state detectable. They do not make the output correct.

The historical QA evaluation illustrates the distinction. Extractive composition largely avoided unsupported statements by copying supported passages, yet claim--citation choice and strict answer-point coverage were much weaker: only \QaCoveredAnswerPointCount{}/\QaAnswerPointCount{} points were covered (\QaAnswerPointCoverage{}). It selected off-topic evidence and omitted requested information even when every sentence carried a locator. The prospective run separately tests the repaired selective-response and exact-locator contracts; its citation precision is \VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecision{} and answer-point coverage \VTwoQAAnswerPointCoverage{} (\VTwoQAAnswerPointCoverageCi{}). These historical and prospective estimates share the frozen technical snapshot but are non-paired and only descriptively comparable because the cases, denominators, locator and answer constructs, and protocols differ. Neither measure turns a matching locator into entailment. Traceability reduces the cost of diagnosis; it does not substitute for diagnosis.

\section{Simple lexical retrieval remained competitive}

Keyword retrieval produced the highest held-out MRR and nDCG@10 point estimates. Learned dense was close, but the study was not designed as an equivalence or non-inferiority test. The tuned hybrid ranked below both and did not yield a corrected significant gain even over feature hashing. This agrees with the broader lesson of heterogeneous retrieval benchmarks: learned representations are not automatically superior for every corpus \cite{thakur2021beir}.

Several features may explain the keyword result without establishing causality. The laboratory questions and paper titles share specialised vocabulary; relevant-paper labels may favour explicit lexical matches; the corpus is small; and the candidate pool is already rich. The post-selection ablation showing a stronger cumulative configuration is hypothesis-generating, not an invitation to retune on the held-out set. A future release needs a larger preregistered test partition and hard paraphrase cases.

\section{Selective response is prospectively re-evaluated}

Historical v1 retrieval returned a positive result on its lone held-out unanswerable case. In the historical QA set, the answerer returned \QaResponseCount{} responses and \QaAbstentionCount{} abstention overall, abstaining on \QaUnanswerableAbstentionCount{}/\QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases. The repaired fixed coverage gate is evaluated prospectively: answerability precision is \VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecision{} with false-positive rate \VTwoQAFalsePositiveRate{} and abstention rate \VTwoQAAbstentionRate{}. Although the frozen technical snapshot is shared, the prospective cases, denominators, and answerability protocol differ, so this is not a paired before/after effect estimate. It is bounded evidence about the implemented selective-response contract, and the set remains small and source-derived. A later protocol should expand adversarial and out-of-corpus cases and report risk jointly with retained answer coverage.

\section{Recommendation structure is stronger than recommendation validation}

The extension schema separates baseline evidence, explicit versus inferred gaps, system suggestions, MVP/stretch scope, risk, skills, data assumptions, and evaluation plans. Historical review found that the evidence-only arm filled \RecommendationEvidenceReturnedCount{}/\RecommendationRequestedSlotCount{} requested ranks but the full Finder filled only \RecommendationFullReturnedCount{}/\RecommendationRequestedSlotCount{}, with \RecommendationFullMissingCount{} missing slots scored as zero. Full-Finder AI-proxy relevance was \RecommendationFullRelevance{} versus \RecommendationEvidenceRelevance{} for the baseline, a paired change of \RecommendationRelevanceDifference{} (\RecommendationDifferenceLow{}--\RecommendationDifferenceHigh{}); improvement was not demonstrated. Conditional on the \RecommendationFullReturnedCount{} emitted items, separation passed \RecommendationSeparationPassCount{}, feasibility failed \RecommendationFeasibilityFailCount{}, and AI-proxy usefulness failed \RecommendationUsefulnessFailCount{}. These failures mean that the evidence did not establish those properties, not that every proposed project is infeasible or useless. The prospective paired test measures a separate ranking and contract protocol: its Hit@3 change is \VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDelta{} (\VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDeltaCi{}), while fact/suggestion separation is \VTwoFinderFactSuggestionSeparation{} and unknown-time retention \VTwoFinderUnknownTimeRetention{}. Neither protocol establishes student usefulness, novelty, real feasibility, or supervisor fit. The structured Finder remains an evaluated brainstorming aid, and evidence-only mode lets a cautious user stop before generated advice.

The newer conversational Idea Generator improves interaction and failure transparency, not the frozen effectiveness result. Its schema, alias validation, paper-informed/general basis, strict local-provider policy, and transient history reduce specific presentation and provenance risks. The four formative prompts and automated tests establish that these code paths behave as specified under fixtures. They do not show that the generated directions are better than Finder output, novel, feasible, or useful to students. Treating the feature as impressive engineering while preserving this limit is more defensible than transferring the Finder metrics to it.

\section{Controlled discovery favors inspectability}

Historical topic comparison exposed a practical trade-off: the dense prototype increased coverage/recall but reduced precision, while controlled rules were deterministic and editable. Revalidation retained \TopicExactBindingCount{} exact chunk bindings and allowed \TopicReboundBindingCount{} conservative rebound only after a unique same-paper locator and exact excerpt match. That exception addresses identifier drift across distinct snapshots; it does not collapse the snapshots or independently re-adjudicate the topic label. The prospective positive-only set instead asks whether known positives are recovered, yielding recall \VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecall{} while leaving \VTwoTopicUnadjudicatedPredictionCount{} additional predictions unadjudicated. It cannot confirm precision or expertise. The remaining \PossibleAuthorMergeCount{} identity candidates show why automatic name merging is intentionally conservative. Publication-derived relations should remain qualified even after identity resolution.

\section{Review and governance as first-class data}

Append-only AI review events close the earlier empty-governance state and demonstrate deterministic idempotence. The design does not conflate that activity with human approval: reproducible outputs are AI-reviewed, while configuration-incomplete historical answers remain needs-reprocess. Local account sessions, scoped service actors, CSRF checks, chain hashes, and actor-bound preview/execute operations make future review attributable and reject stale bulk state. A deployed institution still needs operational ownership, identity lifecycle, correction response times, and legal/privacy decisions.

\section{Interface conformance is not human validation}

The requirement--API--view--test trace shows that implemented public and protected flows preserve warnings, request-lifecycle boundaries, authorization, and correction state under tested conditions. At the evaluated snapshot the strict public projection is empty, which makes fail-closed behavior testable but provides no evidence about a populated production experience. The labelled local preview improves demonstration access but is not a rights decision. A semantic label can exist while remaining confusing; keyboard focus can be visible while a screen-reader workflow remains inefficient; and an Idea Generator warning can render while a student still over-trusts the suggestion. RQ7 therefore closes an engineering traceability gap without converting automated conformance into usability, accessibility, trust, or educational evidence.

\section{Reproducibility with rights constraints}

An exact full-corpus replication requires lawful access to the third-party papers; publishing their text would improve convenience by violating the evidential and rights boundary. The sanitized release therefore favours computational transparency over unrestricted data redistribution. It includes code, schemas, labels, sanitized results, prompts/configurations, hashes, code-native figure sources, and instructions, while requiring recipients to reacquire permitted documents. Interface screenshots are excluded because rendered source/answer text cannot be field-redacted reliably. The external sanity harness mapped open JATS/XML documents into the production chunker's extraction contract. It did not exercise the primary PDF-ingestion path, and a few life-science articles do not establish domain transfer.
