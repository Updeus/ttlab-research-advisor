\section{RQ1: corpus, extraction, and index integrity}

The catalog contains \CatalogPaperCount{} records. \AvailablePdfCount{} PDFs were available; \EligiblePaperCount{} papers passed the frozen inclusion rules, \NoTextPaperCount{} had no eligible full text, and \MismatchPaperCount{} were excluded for suspected PDF/title mismatch. The raw database contains \RawChunkCount{} chunks; \EligibleChunkCount{} are eligible, and \EligibleUnknownChunkCount{} of those retain an unknown section. The \SectionSilverCaseCount{}-case AI-silver section review produced exact accuracy \SectionSilverAccuracy{}, labeled coverage 0.900, macro precision \SectionSilverMacroPrecision{}, and macro recall \SectionSilverMacroRecall{}. The unknown-section share remains material, so section-aware ranking is a fallible signal.

Keyword, feature-hashing, and learned-dense indexes each cover \EligibleChunkCount{}/\EligibleChunkCount{} eligible chunks (\cref{fig:index-coverage}). Their manifests share corpus snapshot \texttt{\CorpusSnapshotId}; the feature-hashing and dense index SHA-256 values are \texttt{f233...fa5} and \texttt{ac0d...6a3}. The prior partial-index/stale-status defect is therefore closed for the frozen corpus. The bounded conclusion for RQ1 is that complete derived-state coverage is achieved for the eligible subset, while 38 catalog records remain outside that subset and 199 eligible chunks lack a confident section label.
