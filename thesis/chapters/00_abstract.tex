\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Publication archives expose bibliographic records but not full-text discovery, evidence-linked question answering, author/topic exploration, or bounded thesis-extension design. This thesis designs and evaluates a source-traceable research-intelligence platform for a TTLAB corpus. Here, source-traceable means that paper, chunk, page, section, provider, timestamp, warning, and review-state identifiers persist through retrieval and generation; it does not imply factual correctness or entailment.

The artefact-oriented methodology uses source-derived AI-reviewed silver evidence. Its frozen technical snapshot contains \CatalogPaperCount{} catalog records, \EligiblePaperCount{} eligible full-text papers, and \EligibleChunkCount{} eligible chunks after excluding \MismatchPaperCount{} PDF/title mismatches. Keyword, feature-hashing, and pinned learned-dense indexes cover the complete eligible set. The independent publication-and-rights gate is stricter: no paper in the audited snapshot was approved for public search, so anonymous Search, Ask, Finder, and Explorer fail closed rather than substitute technical records.

Historical v1 evidence retains strict answer-point coverage of \QaCoveredAnswerPointCount{}/\QaAnswerPointCount{} (\QaAnswerPointCoverage{}); \QaResponseCount{} cases received a response, \QaAbstentionCount{} abstained, and only \QaUnanswerableAbstentionCount{}/\QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases abstained. A prospectively frozen protocol evaluated selective QA, paired Finder ranking, positive-only topics, and an OCR fixture. Over \VTwoQATestCases{} held-out cases, answerability precision was \VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecision{} (\VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecisionCi{}) and exact-locator citation precision \VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecision{} (\VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecisionCi{}). Finder Hit@3 changed by \VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDelta{} (\VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDeltaCi{}) over \VTwoFinderTestProfiles{} profiles. Known-positive topic recall was \VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecall{} (\VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecallCi{}) over \VTwoTopicTestCases{} cases; OCR fixture character error was \VTwoOCRCharacterErrorRate{}. V1 and v2 share the technical corpus but differ in cases, denominators, constructs, and protocols, so their comparison is descriptive, not paired. These \VTwoEvidenceTier{} observations are not human validation, entailment, public effectiveness, novelty, feasibility, or student benefit. The contribution is an evidence-calibrated platform and reproducible evaluation package, not a validated research advisor.
