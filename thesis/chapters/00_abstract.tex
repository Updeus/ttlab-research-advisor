\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Publication archives expose bibliographic records but not full-text discovery, evidence-linked question answering, content summarization, author/topic exploration, or bounded research-idea design. This thesis designs and evaluates a source-traceable research-intelligence platform for a TTLAB corpus. Its evaluated structured Thesis Extension Finder is complemented by a post-evaluation conversational Idea Generator that returns one to three schema-validated directions and distinguishes paper-informed output from general suggestions. Here, source-traceable means that paper, chunk, page, section, provider, timestamp, warning, and review-state identifiers persist through retrieval and generation; it does not imply factual correctness or entailment.

The artefact-oriented methodology uses source-derived AI-reviewed silver evidence. Its frozen technical snapshot contains \CatalogPaperCount{} catalog records, \EligiblePaperCount{} eligible full-text papers, and \EligibleChunkCount{} eligible chunks after excluding \MismatchPaperCount{} PDF/title mismatches. Keyword, feature-hashing, and pinned learned-dense indexes cover the complete eligible set. The independent production publication-and-rights gate is stricter: no paper in the audited snapshot was approved for public search. An explicit loopback-only demo can preview technically eligible records with visible rights/review warnings, but it does not change publication decisions.

Historical v1 evidence retains strict answer-point coverage of \QaCoveredAnswerPointCount{}/\QaAnswerPointCount{} (\QaAnswerPointCoverage{}); \QaResponseCount{} cases received a response, \QaAbstentionCount{} abstained, and only \QaUnanswerableAbstentionCount{}/\QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases abstained. A prospectively frozen protocol evaluated selective QA, paired Finder ranking, positive-only topics, and an OCR fixture. Over \VTwoQATestCases{} held-out cases, answerability precision was \VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecision{} (\VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecisionCi{}) and exact-locator citation precision \VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecision{} (\VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecisionCi{}). Finder Hit@3 changed by \VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDelta{} (\VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDeltaCi{}) over \VTwoFinderTestProfiles{} profiles. Known-positive topic recall was \VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecall{} (\VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecallCi{}) over \VTwoTopicTestCases{} cases; OCR fixture character error was \VTwoOCRCharacterErrorRate{}. V1 and v2 share the technical corpus but differ in cases, denominators, constructs, and protocols, so their comparison is descriptive, not paired. The newer Idea Generator has deterministic contract tests and four formative regression prompts, but no live-model quality or human-usefulness evaluation. These observations are not human validation, entailment, public effectiveness, novelty, feasibility, or student benefit. The contribution is an evidence-calibrated platform and reproducible evaluation package, not a validated research advisor.
