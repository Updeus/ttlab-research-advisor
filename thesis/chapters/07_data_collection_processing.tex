\section{Corpus provenance}

The seed contains 134 publication records derived from a TTLAB WordPress archive and matches the saved discovery JSON byte-for-byte. Its SHA-256 digest is recorded in \codepath{PROJECT_EVIDENCE.md}. The snapshot includes local PDFs for 98 papers; 35 are missing and one download is marked failed. \authorinput{confirm the collection date, archive completeness, laboratory authorization, and copyright/licensing basis for retaining PDFs and displaying snippets}.

\section{Ingestion workflow}

\Cref{fig:ingestion} reconstructs the implemented path. Discovery parses archive metadata and stable identifiers. Manual import upserts paper and raw author records. The downloader accepts direct PDF-like URLs and checks content type and the PDF signature. PyMuPDF extracts text page by page and records counts, empty pages, a text hash, and low-text warnings. A sentence heuristic then forms page-aware, overlapping chunks.

\section{Chunking}

The default chunk target is 850 words with a maximum of 5,000 characters and 125 overlapping words. A final chunk shorter than 400 characters is merged with its predecessor. Each identifier incorporates paper ID, sequence, page span, and content hash. Sections are inferred by matching headings within the first 1,200 characters of a chunk; no document-layout model is used.

The 98 extracted papers contain 698 pages, 455,601 source words, and 756 chunks. Overlap raises the sum of chunk words to 547,628, a ratio of 1.202. Of 756 chunks, 249 (32.94\%) have section \texttt{Unknown}. The dominant recognized sections are Methodology (242) and Results (102). Section-sensitive reranking and artefact generation therefore operate over incomplete structural labels.

\section{Metadata quality}

All papers require review. No structured DOI, abstract, keyword, or denormalized paper-topic field is populated in the database; normalized topics are stored separately. Six venues and 93 source URLs are missing. Raw author strings yield 126 records, including separate identities for ``Patrick Hosein'' and ``P. Hosein'', and the parsing artefact ``Click to View'' is linked to 12 papers. Counts of authors and expertise connections must consequently be interpreted as unvalidated string-level records.

\section{Data minimization and retention}

The system stores publication documents and public scholarly metadata rather than user accounts. Recommendation requests can nevertheless contain interests, skills, and constraints; persisted requests may be personal data. No retention schedule, consent notice, access control, or deletion workflow is implemented. Public deployment requires decisions on these issues before recommendation inputs are retained.
