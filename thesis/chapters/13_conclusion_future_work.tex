\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

This thesis designed, repaired, and evaluated a source-traceable research-intelligence platform for a laboratory publication corpus. The system represents \CatalogPaperCount{} catalog records, admits \EligiblePaperCount{} papers and \EligibleChunkCount{} chunks to the frozen experimental corpus, and maintains complete keyword, feature-hashing, and learned-dense authoritative indexes. It exposes search, citation-bearing QA, evidence-only and full thesis-extension workflows, controlled topic/author exploration, generated paper artifacts, evaluation state, and authenticated review through a responsive public interface.

The answers to the research questions are:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=16mm,style=multiline]
  \item[RQ1] The pipeline achieves complete authoritative index coverage for all \EligibleChunkCount{} eligible chunks, excludes \MismatchPaperCount{} suspected document mismatches, and records \NoTextPaperCount{} no-text records. Section labeling reached \SectionSilverAccuracy{} AI-silver accuracy, but \EligibleUnknownChunkCount{} eligible chunks remain unknown-section.
  \item[RQ2] Keyword retrieval had the highest held-out point estimates (set Recall@3 \RetrievalKeywordRecallThree{}; MRR \RetrievalKeywordMrr{}); learned dense produced \RetrievalDenseRecallThree{} and \RetrievalDenseMrr{}, respectively. The study did not establish equivalence between those modes, and corrected paired tests did not demonstrate tuned-hybrid superiority.
  \item[RQ3] Offline extraction yielded local claim support \QaSupportedClaimRate{} but only \QaCitationCorrectness{} citation correctness, \QaAnswerPointCoverage{} answer-point coverage, and no abstention on \QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases. Source traceability therefore did not imply complete or appropriately scoped answers.
  \item[RQ4] The finder preserved fact/future-work/gap/suggestion boundaries, but the \RecommendationRelevanceDifference{} relevance difference over an evidence-only baseline had a 95\% CI spanning zero and every feasibility judgment remained partial. The controlled lexical taxonomy had higher held-out precision than the dense prototype; author auditing removed known parser contamination without unsafe merges; and every stored generated output received attributable AI review or needs-reprocess status. These are inspectable advisory/discovery outputs, not validated thesis advice, endorsement, or human approval.
  \item[RQ5] Engineering evidence supports local reproducibility and reviewability under the documented configuration, protected evidence interfaces, and a privacy-minimizing release that excludes restricted PDFs. Automated accessibility regression checks passed, but they do not establish WCAG conformance or assistive-technology usability. The external sanity harness mapped three CC BY JATS/XML articles into the production chunker contract and achieved 3/3 fixed lexical top-one matches; it did not validate the main PDF-ingestion pipeline or cross-domain retrieval quality. Performance is characterised on one machine at concurrency one; production capacity and broad external validity remain unestablished.
\end{description}

The principal conclusion is deliberately restrained: the project provides a rigorous, inspectable research-intelligence artefact and evidence package, but not a human-validated research advisor. Its most important empirical finding is the gap between traceability and utility: supported, fully cited extractive claims can still be incomplete, weakly matched to citations, and inappropriate for out-of-corpus questions.

\section{Prioritised future work}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Calibrate abstention.} Expand unanswerable and adversarial cases, learn or calibrate evidence/score thresholds on development data, and measure selective risk and coverage.
  \item \textbf{Improve evidence selection.} Use answer-point-aware reranking and within-paper passage retrieval, then re-evaluate citation correctness and content coverage without tuning on the present test set.
  \item \textbf{Run independent human studies.} After institutional ethics determination, recruit appropriate students, supervisors, researchers, and public users to evaluate relevance, comprehension, feasibility, trust, accessibility, and workflow utility.
  \item \textbf{Resolve corpus and identity gaps.} Acquire only permitted missing PDFs, externally verify mismatch documents and candidate author merges, populate provenance-backed metadata, and evaluate OCR on a real scanned-document subset.
  \item \textbf{Replicate externally and at scale.} Apply the frozen protocol to additional authorized laboratories, disciplines, and languages; enlarge the test set; and conduct concurrent load/endurance testing in a production-like deployment.
  \item \textbf{Strengthen operational governance.} Integrate institutional identity, documented correction and appeal processes, retention/deletion controls, monitoring, backups, incident response, and independent security/accessibility assessment.
\end{enumerate}

Venue PDF validation, official declaration language, conflict/funding attestations, and institutional policy confirmation are external submission steps. They are kept outside the manuscript's empirical claims rather than filled with unsupported text.
