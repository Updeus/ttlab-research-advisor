\section{Technical and empirical limitations}

Only \EligiblePaperCount{} of \CatalogPaperCount{} records enter full-text experiments; \NoTextPaperCount{} lack text and \MismatchPaperCount{} mismatches require review. Extraction is heuristic, corpus OCR was not run, and the fixture is not scanned-corpus accuracy. \EligibleUnknownChunkCount{} chunks lack sections, metadata are incomplete, \PossibleAuthorMergeCount{} identities remain unresolved, and the empty public projection supplies no populated-public evidence.

Historical v1 retrieval has 50 AI-silver cases and 20 held out, including one unanswerable; pools may omit relevance, unjudged documents count negative, and ablation was post-selection. Historical QA covered only \QaCoveredAnswerPointCount{}/\QaAnswerPointCount{} strict points and contains \QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases, while historical Finder comparison includes \RecommendationFullMissingCount{} unfilled full-Finder ranks scored as zero. Its item-level judgments are conditional on the \RecommendationFullReturnedCount{} emitted outputs. Prospective sets are also small, source-derived and technical-only. Historical and prospective QA results share the frozen technical snapshot but use different cases, denominators, locator and answer constructs, and protocols, so they are descriptive rather than paired before/after estimates. All intervals omit label and corpus-selection uncertainty.

QA uses one extractive answerer and one AI-review process; high historical support partly reflects copying, and prospective results cover only frozen cases. Ollama was unavailable, so no generative comparison exists. Recommendation and topic judgments remain AI-proxy/silver, not user judgments or human labels. A feasibility or usefulness failure records absence of supporting evidence under the rubric; it is not a human determination that a project is infeasible or useless. Single-machine concurrency-one performance is not capacity testing.

\section{Ethics, permissions, and responsible AI}

TTLAB explicitly authorized the project and use of the laboratory corpus. This fact does not establish research-ethics-board approval or exemption, ownership of third-party publications, or blanket redistribution permission. No human participants were recruited; no satisfaction, usability, educational benefit, or supervisor-approval claim is made. If a later human study is undertaken, institutional ethics determination, informed consent, recruitment, retention, compensation, and withdrawal procedures must precede data collection.

Potential harms include source misrepresentation, inappropriate thesis advice, expertise misattribution, copyright overexposure, privacy leakage from project profiles, malicious documents, and unauthorised review changes. Controls include source locators, fact/inference/suggestion labels, AI/human review separation, warnings, evidence-only mode, transient profiles, authenticated mutations, append-only event hashes, PDF/downloader limits, and a sanitized release. These controls reduce risk; they do not amount to institutional approval or human verification.

Codex/LLM assistance was material in coding, evidence inspection, test and evaluation preparation, AI-silver labeling, analysis, and manuscript editing. Reviewer IDs, prompts or prompt hashes, seeds, and limitations are persisted. The exact underlying Codex model digest was not exposed by the runtime and was not invented. This disclosure should be reconciled with the University's current submission policy by the author/institution before formal submission.

\section{Construct validity}

Set Recall@$k$, Hit@$k$, MRR, nDCG, citation correctness, citation completeness, supported-claim rate, answer-point coverage, and abstention are defined separately. ``Grounded'' in operational records denotes structural checks, not entailment. Topic score denotes controlled or prototype label evidence, not topic truth. Recommendation score denotes heuristic fit, not usefulness, novelty, or feasibility. AI-review consistency is not human inter-rater reliability. Index coverage and test counts are engineering properties, not effectiveness.

\section{Internal validity}

Development/test separation and prospective pre-execution case/split freezing reduce leakage. Nevertheless, source-derived labels came from one AI-assisted process and may reflect corpus vocabulary; the same AI reviewer verified them. Historical topic revalidation includes \TopicReboundBindingCount{} conservative locator rebound across distinct snapshots, guarded by unique same-paper location and exact bounded-excerpt matching; this preserves a label binding but does not independently re-adjudicate it. Error tags are non-causal, and WSL load/caches affect performance.

\section{External validity}

The system and controlled taxonomy are tailored to one laboratory, English-language records, and a small local corpus. Archive markup, disciplinary terminology, PDF quality, and user goals will differ elsewhere. The three-document Europe PMC check covers only CC BY life-science JATS/XML and trivial lexical queries. It cannot support claims about other institutions, languages, domains, or large corpora. No human study supports generalisation to student decision-making or public understanding.

\section{Reproducibility validity}

Code, structural results, model/environment identities, seeds, and manifests are versioned and bind active index generations. The historical topic receipt distinguishes \TopicExactBindingCount{} exact identities from \TopicReboundBindingCount{} excerpt-verified rebound rather than presenting a rebuilt snapshot as the original. Exact replication still needs lawful sources, model availability and compatible tools. Restricted-raw hashes attest runner observations, not independently inspectable content; open analytical and conditional full-corpus reproduction remain distinct.

\section{Security and deployment limitations}

The repository provides a fail-closed production configuration boundary, but it is not a hosted service. TLS, reverse proxy, identity-provider lifecycle, a distributed rate limiter, central monitoring, backups, incident response staffing, legal notices, accessibility conformance assessment, and operational correction processes remain deployment-specific. Scheduled corpus synchronization is deliberately disabled until whole-snapshot atomic promotion exists.

The current Tectonic thesis PDF is untagged. Semantic source structure, text alternatives in figure captions/descriptions, bookmarks, selectable text, and embedded fonts improve portability, but they do not establish PDF/UA conformance. A tagged-PDF-capable toolchain and independent accessibility inspection are required before making that claim. External IEEE PDF Express/Checker validation and any venue/institutional declarations likewise require credentials and authorized human attestations.
