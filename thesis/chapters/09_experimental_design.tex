\section{Retrieval configuration}

The retrieval dataset contains all eleven specified strata, including four out-of-corpus cases overall. The deterministic split assigns \RetrievalDevCount{} cases to development and \RetrievalTestCount{} to test; the held-out set contains \RetrievalAnswerableTestCount{} answerable and \RetrievalUnanswerableTestCount{} unanswerable case. All methods use the \texttt{\CorpusSnapshotId} corpus, top 150 chunks per component for frozen candidate pools, a top-50 chunk depth, and top ten unique papers. Unjudged papers count as non-relevant.

Development tuning searched nine keyword/dense weight pairs from 0.10/0.80 to 0.80/0.10, with a 0.42/0.48 implementation reference. The objective was development MRR with fixed secondary rules. The selected tuned configuration used keyword 0.10, dense 0.80, and all heuristic adjustments enabled. Sensitivity tested nearby 0.05/0.85, 0.15/0.75, 0.20/0.70, and 0.30/0.60 weights. Individual ablations removed one component from the selected hybrid; cumulative ablations added components in the documented order.

\section{Historical v1 QA review procedure}

The historical offline extractive evaluation contained \QaCaseCount{} reviewed cases: the answerer returned \QaResponseCount{} responses and \QaAbstentionCount{} abstention. Each atomic corpus claim was linked to inline source IDs and returned citation records. The AI reviewer performed a support-first creation pass and a shuffled contradiction-focused verification pass; the verification label was final, with disagreements retained. The metrics use strict treatment of partial labels. Confidence intervals resample whole QA cases 10,000 times, preserving within-case claim correlation. They do not include AI-label uncertainty.

An Ollama comparison was attempted at the configured local endpoint on port 11434 during the frozen historical evaluation. CLI version 0.21.2 was present, but version, tags, and process endpoints refused connections. No installed model, digest, quantisation, response, or latency could be verified, so that comparison is reported as unavailable rather than approximated. The subsequently implemented Idea Generator is therefore outside the frozen QA/Finder result sets.

\section{Post-evaluation Idea Generator verification boundary}

The Idea Generator adds an engineering-verification layer rather than a new scientific experiment. Four source-derived formative cases cover a RAG-oriented request, an agriculture-oriented request, an out-of-corpus request, and a multi-turn scope refinement. They were created to exercise request/schema, source-alias, paper-match, and conversation contracts; they are not gold recommendations, student ratings, or held-out evidence of usefulness. Backend tests stub the local provider to verify retrieval depth, answerability gating, alias rejection, paper-informed/general classification, provenance, digest-drift failure, structured-output repair, and retryable errors. Frontend tests verify transient conversation, citations, warnings, provider metadata, offline states, and retry. A future effectiveness protocol must freeze new cases before model/prompt selection and obtain appropriately authorised independent human judgments.

\section{Historical v1 recommendation, topic, and review protocols}

The \RecommendationProfileCount{} synthetic profiles span one-month, two-week, and semester timelines; software, ML, analysis, dashboard, literature-support, and other project types; public/synthetic/no-external/supervisor data constraints; easy/medium/hard preferences; and avoid topics. Each arm requested three ranks, for \RecommendationRequestedSlotCount{} slots. The evidence-only arm returned \RecommendationEvidenceReturnedCount{}, whereas safe filtering left the full Finder with \RecommendationFullReturnedCount{} returned items and \RecommendationFullMissingCount{} unfilled slots; paired top-$k$ relevance assigns zero to every missing slot rather than evaluating only emitted recommendations. Review criteria are conditional on returned items and were applied in two shuffled AI passes. The paired relevance contrast and sensitivity results resample profiles, not individual recommendations.

The topic dataset contains 36 development and 24 test papers, covers all controlled labels across the full dataset, includes 51 multi-label cases and four \texttt{other/unknown} cases. The dense threshold (0.20) was selected by development micro-F1, with macro-F1 and higher threshold as tie-breakers. Test data were not used for selection. Revalidation bound \TopicExactBindingCount{} labels by exact chunk identity and conservatively rebound \TopicReboundBindingCount{} by a unique same-paper page/section/chunk-index locator plus exact bounded-excerpt match. Because the frozen label snapshot and rebuilt technical snapshot are distinct, this is a documented locator-preservation check, not evidence that the underlying texts or topic judgments are interchangeable. The author audit checks only source-backed invariants; candidate merges are preserved for external identity confirmation.

Generated-output review covers every stored RAG answer, recommendation run, and paper artifact. The procedure records rationale, citation locators, corrections, actor type, and chained event hashes. Configuration-incomplete historical outputs remain \texttt{needs\_reprocess}; they are not deleted or silently regenerated.

\section{Performance and reproducibility protocol}

The full benchmark uses three process-cold and three warm repetitions at concurrency one. Each workload runs in an isolated database/index/output context and retains raw timing and bounded errors. The operating-system cache is not flushed. A linearly interpolated p95 over three observations is coarse and is reported only as a local baseline. The one-command reproduction path verifies dependencies, rebuilds allowed derived state, runs evaluations and tests, compiles both manuscripts, performs PDF preflight, builds the sanitized release, and emits hashes. Exact full-corpus reruns require lawful access to local PDFs and the pinned dense model.
