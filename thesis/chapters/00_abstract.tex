\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Publication archives expose bibliographic records but do not directly support full-text discovery, evidence-linked question answering, author/topic exploration, or the formulation of bounded thesis extensions. This thesis presents the design and controlled offline evaluation of a source-traceable research-intelligence platform for a TTLAB laboratory corpus. Source-traceable means that paper, chunk, page, section, provider, timestamp, warning, and review-state identifiers persist through retrieval and generated-output workflows; it does not mean that every claim is factually correct or entailed.

The artefact-oriented methodology uses source-derived AI-reviewed silver evidence. Its frozen technical snapshot contains \CatalogPaperCount{} catalog records, \EligiblePaperCount{} eligible full-text papers, and \EligibleChunkCount{} eligible chunks after excluding \MismatchPaperCount{} PDF/title mismatches. Keyword, feature-hashing, and pinned learned-dense indexes cover the complete eligible set. The independent publication-and-rights gate is stricter: no paper in the audited snapshot was approved for public search, so anonymous Search, Ask, Finder, and Explorer fail closed rather than substitute technical records.

Immutable historical v1 evidence retains the negative diagnosis that traceable extractive answers can still omit requested points and choose imperfect citations: strict answer-point coverage was \QaCoveredAnswerPointCount{}/\QaAnswerPointCount{} (\QaAnswerPointCoverage{}), while \QaResponseCount{} cases received a response, \QaAbstentionCount{} abstained, and only \QaUnanswerableAbstentionCount{}/\QaUnanswerableCaseCount{} unanswerable cases abstained. A prospectively frozen protocol then evaluated selective QA, paired Finder ranking, positive-only topics, and an OCR fixture. Over \VTwoQATestCases{} held-out cases, answerability precision was \VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecision{} (\VTwoQAAnswerabilityPrecisionCi{}) and exact-locator citation precision \VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecision{} (\VTwoQACitationLocatorPrecisionCi{}). Finder Hit@3 changed by \VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDelta{} (\VTwoFinderHitAtThreeDeltaCi{}) over \VTwoFinderTestProfiles{} profiles. Known-positive topic recall was \VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecall{} (\VTwoTopicKnownPositiveRecallCi{}) over \VTwoTopicTestCases{} cases; OCR fixture character error was \VTwoOCRCharacterErrorRate{}. Historical and prospective QA estimates are descriptive, not paired changes, because their cases, protocols, and technical snapshots differ. These \VTwoEvidenceTier{} technical observations are not human validation, entailment, public effectiveness, novelty, feasibility, or student benefit. The contribution is an evidence-calibrated platform and reproducible evaluation package, not a validated research advisor.
