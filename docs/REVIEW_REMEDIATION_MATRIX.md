# Review Remediation Matrix

## Purpose and status vocabulary

This is the authoritative closure register for the July 2026 overall flaws and
limitations assessment, `AUTHOR_REVIEW.md`, and the requirements in
`CODEX_MASTER_REMEDIATION_PROMPT.md`. The baseline was rechecked against
`origin/main` at `ce6d805afba27c2550da2ca58d1f844793ea25a8`; the remediation
branch initially differed only by the committed master prompt at
`649244552f4d68fc49bcc6d35a94e99e4e638dd8`.

Statuses have precise meanings:

- `confirmed / pending`: reproduced at baseline and not yet remediated;
- `already_fixed`: the earlier finding no longer reproduces and current evidence
  demonstrates the correction;
- `changed since audit / pending`: the original evidence changed, but the
  underlying requirement is still open;
- `closed`: implemented and verified by the listed evidence;
- `mitigated by claim reduction`: the software limitation remains, but the
  unsupported claim has been removed and the residual limitation is explicit;
- `external-only`: only an institution, rights-holder, venue, or named author
  can legitimately supply the remaining attestation.

No item is marked closed from a plan, source inspection, or passing unit test
alone when the requirement calls for executed experiments or rendered
artefacts. The final distribution is 87 `closed`, one `mitigated by claim
reduction` (`RAG-01`), and one `external-only` (`PAPER-14`). Closure of the
`REP-*` and `RELEASE-*` rows is delivery-conditional: the exact committed
candidate must pass the fail-loud commands recorded below. If any such command
or checksum/manifest/exclusion check fails, those rows revert to pending and the
delivery is not complete.

## External-only attestations tracked separately

The following are deliberately not treated as software defects or filled with
invented values: student ID; supervisor; official programme/degree and
submission date; university declaration/copyright/acknowledgements; official
TTLAB expansion; REC/IRB approval or exemption; funding and conflicts; venue
credentials/PDF eXpress; institutional AI-use approval; rights-holder permission
to redistribute third-party PDFs; supervisor approval; and any claim of human
usefulness. They must appear in `docs/EXTERNAL_SUBMISSION_CHECKS.md` and are
eligible only for `external-only` closure.
