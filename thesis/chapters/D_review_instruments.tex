\chapter{Proposed Review Instruments}
\label{app:review-instruments}

These instruments are proposed and have not been administered. They require ethics and supervisor approval.

\section{Answer and citation review}

For each atomic answer claim, record: supported/partially supported/unsupported; correct paper; correct page/chunk; citation entails claim; important context omitted; conflicting evidence; and reviewer notes. At answer level, rate faithfulness and usefulness on a defined five-point rubric and record whether fallback occurred.

\section{Recommendation review}

Review source fidelity separately from the suggestion. Rate paper relevance, gap classification, novelty, feasibility within stated time, data accessibility, skill calibration, MVP clarity, risk calibration, and evaluation-plan appropriateness. Reviewers should flag where a system inference is phrased as if it appeared in the paper.

\section{Topic and author review}

For each sampled paper, mark applicable controlled topics, missing topics, incorrect topics, and ambiguous labels. For author records, first decide identity merges/splits and parsing errors, then review whether topic evidence supports a qualified expertise association.

\section{Minimum provenance fields}

Each judgment should retain case ID, corpus/index version, output ID, provider/model/prompt version, reviewer pseudonym, timestamp, decision, rationale, and adjudication state. \authorinput{approve instruments, scales, reviewer training, anonymity, retention, and adjudication procedure}.
